api_execution_live_gpay_activity

Dashboard-facing feed of recent Gnosis Pay Payments and Fiat Top Ups on Gnosis Chain. Reads from `int_live__gpay_activity_raw` (last 30 minutes of cached data, HWM-anchored, 60-second reorg buffer). No LIMIT — callers should paginate. Balances / retention / Celo are out of scope.
