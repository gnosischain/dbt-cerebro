fct_celo_gpay_card_funding

Deterministic funding-relationship per card Safe: which addresses fund it (inbound transfers), per token, plus each funder's fan-out and a label-free funding_channel. RAW addresses, NO identity labels — we do not claim a funder "is" MiniPay, a ramp, or a CEX. Grain (safe_address, funder, token_address). All-token (the marts whitelist scope comment does not apply). funding_channel is a SHAPE classification from int_celo_gpay_funding_tx_envelopes (transaction envelope + call pattern + funder fan-out). CIP-64 (transaction_type 123) alone is not MiniPay — most of Celo uses that envelope. Channel values: cip64_direct_solo — every transfer in this relationship is CIP-64, the funder EOA called token.transfer() directly, and that funder funds exactly one card. This is the MiniPay-shaped path; do not rename the value to "minipay". other_direct — same direct EOA transfer() shape, but the envelope is not CIP-64 (type 0/2), and the funder funds exactly one card. hub — this funder funds two or more cards. Fan-out wins over envelope: a multi-card CIP-64 funder is still hub. Includes the legacy 0xa0dd… hub (type 0, ~250 cards). mediated — not a direct EOA→token transfer (e.g. Gnosis Safe execTransaction 0x6a761202, routers). Assigned before hub when the call is not direct. mixed — transfers in this (safe, funder, token) relationship disagree on channel (rare). unknown — transfer row(s) could not be joined to celo_execution.transactions.
