int_celo_gpay_funder_wallet_daily

Daily funding-wallet activity at (date, wallet, direction, counterparty_class, token) grain, folded off int_celo_gpay_funder_wallet_transfers. FEE LEGS ARE EXCLUDED HERE, ON PURPOSE, AND THIS IS THE MODEL DOWNSTREAM SHOULD USE. A CIP-64 fee Transfer is the gas receipt of an action, not an action; counting it makes one payment look like two. "Active" in the wallet-side tree therefore means: moved a token that was not a gas fee.
