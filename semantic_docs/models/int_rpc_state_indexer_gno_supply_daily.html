int_rpc_state_indexer_gno_supply_daily

Daily GNO supply breakdown derived from indexer on-chain state, replicating the Dune GNO-supply label contract — Gnosis Circ. is the chain-100 GNO totalSupply, Non-Circ. sums the three vesting-wallet ETH-GNO balances, and Ethereum Circ. is the minted chain-1 totalSupply minus GNO burned to the zero address (~3.15M) minus vesting minus the Omnibridge escrow. Each series is dedupped per day and forward-filled per label. Verified exact against the Dune series over the full overlap except single-day timing artifacts on days a tracked wallet moved after the census day-anchor block (state-at-anchor vs Dune's end-of-day transfer sum; self-corrects the next day).
