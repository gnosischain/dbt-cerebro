api_gno_supply_daily

Daily GNO supply by category label served from the rpc-state-indexer derivation; the api:gno_supply endpoint (succeeding the Dune-fed api_crawlers_data_gno_supply_daily, deprecated). Carries three component labels plus a derived 'Total Circ. Supply' — see the label column description before aggregating. History starts 2020-08-23 (Gnosis leg; ETH legs and the derived total from 2020-11-01, the chain-1 day-anchor start) — the pre-2020 series remains queryable in the deprecated Dune models.

labelStringSupply bucket. Three are mutually exclusive components of minted supply — 'Ethereum Circ. Supply', 'Gnosis Circ. Supply', 'Non-Circ. Supply'. The fourth, 'Total Circ. Supply', is DERIVED as Ethereum Circ. + Gnosis Circ. (mainnet circulating plus the Gnosis-chain totalSupply that the Omnibridge escrow backs), so summing or stacking all four double-counts the circulating portion — filter by label instead.
supplyFloat64The amount of GNO supply recorded on the given date, measured in GNO token units.
