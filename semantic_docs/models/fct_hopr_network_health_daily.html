fct_hopr_network_health_daily

Daily HOPR network health per network: how many nodes have ever registered on-chain, versus how many are actually up. THE GAP BETWEEN THOSE IS THE POINT. On-chain registration is cumulative and never expires, so a node that ran once in 2023 and vanished still counts in nodes_registered_cumulative forever; the prober and the dashboard's online series measure the live network. The two have diverged every year -- roughly 1.6x apart in 2023 and 3.5x by 2026, as registrations accumulate while the live network shrinks. Both columns sit side by side specifically so a chart cannot show one while implying the other, and neither should ever be labelled "network size" without saying which it is. Coverage is asymmetric. nodes_online_* reaches back to 2023-09 from the dashboard history; the prober columns start only when click-runner's hopr-network ingestor first ran and are NULL before that, meaning unmeasured, never zero. Both prober feeds are dufour-only, so jura carries registration counts and nothing else -- jura's missing liveness is the prober never having been ported to v4, not jura being down.

Columns

NameTypeDescription
avg_availability_24hNullable(Float64)Mean 24-hour availability across all probed nodes, in [0,1].
avg_latency_msNullable(Float64)Mean round-trip time across reachable nodes, milliseconds.
dateDateCalendar day, UTC.
hours_observedUInt64Distinct hours the dashboard actually recorded that day, 0 to 24. The history is far from complete, so this is how a consumer tells a well-observed day from a single-sample one. Zero means the online columns are NULL.
networkStringHOPR network; dufour, or jura for registration counts only.
nodes_high_availabilityNullable(UInt64)Nodes up at least 90% of the last 24 hours. Kept apart from nodes_reachable because a node can answer one probe while being down most of the day.
nodes_online_avgNullable(Float64)Mean of the dashboard's hourly online count across the hours observed that day. NULL where the dashboard history has no hours at all. Always read with hours_observed.
nodes_online_maxNullable(UInt32)Highest hourly online count observed that day.
nodes_online_minNullable(UInt32)Lowest hourly online count observed that day.
nodes_probedNullable(UInt64)Nodes the prober attempted that day. NULL before the ingestor started and for every jura row. Consistently far below nodes_registered_cumulative, because the prober tracks the live set rather than the historical registry.
nodes_reachableNullable(UInt64)Nodes the prober actually reached, i.e. that returned a latency.
nodes_registered_cumulativeUInt64Running total of nodes ever registered on this network up to and including this date. Never decrements -- deregistration and abandonment are invisible to it. This is NOT the live network size.
nodes_registered_newUInt64Nodes whose first KeyBinding fell on this date. KeyBinding is the act of joining the network; AddressAnnouncement repeats whenever a node re-announces an address, so it dates announcements rather than registrations.
p50_latency_msNullable(Float64)Median round-trip time across reachable nodes. Materially below the mean -- a long tail of slow nodes pulls the average up, so the median is the better headline.
