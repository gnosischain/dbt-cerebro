fct_celo_gpay_wallet_share_of_spend_daily

Daily split of what cardholders send OUT of their own wallet: how much went to the GP card, and how much went somewhere else. The competitive question, on-chain. Solo funders only (a hub's outflow is GP onboarding, not a user choosing), priced stablecoins only (token_class from the whitelist seed, never a symbol list). THE OUTLIER SPLIT IS THE WHOLE POINT, DO NOT DROP IT. Over 2026-08-05..08 the raw comparison was $365k sent elsewhere against $48.9k to the card, which reads as the card capturing 12% of its holders' money. It does not: SIX transfers of $1,000+ carried $313.7k of that $365k (largest single $154k - treasury-scale movement by a handful of addresses, not retail behaviour). Excluding them the two sides are ~$51.4k against ~$48.9k, i.e. roughly half. usd_excl_large is the retail-behaviour number, usd_total the flow-of-funds number; they answer different questions and neither is wrong, but mixing them is. Medians over that window were $20.00 to the card against $1.35 elsewhere: the card holds the large ticket while the wallet keeps micro-payments - complement rather than cannibalisation. That is one window; re-check before repeating it.
