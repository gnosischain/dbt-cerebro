fct_celo_gpay_snapshots

All-time and trailing-7D headline snapshots per action (Volume/Count/Users), plus TotalBalance and RewardBalance. Feeds the counter tiles. No Cashback action branch (Gnosis Chain only). TotalBalance is the latest-day SPENDABLE float: mark-to-market USD across token_class = 'STABLECOIN'. RewardBalance is the same for token_class = 'RWA' (zero until the cashback program pays out). The split is driven by celo_tokens_whitelist.token_class, never a hardcoded symbol list, so a newly transacting token cannot be silently excluded from the headline. RWA is reported separately rather than blended because a reward token is not spendable at the settlement contract. FundedCards (added 2026-08-05) counts cards that have ever RECEIVED money and is deliberately distinct from PaymentUsers, which counts cards that have ever SPENT. The two were conflated: api_celo_gpay_total_funded served PaymentUsers, so the funded tile read 662 when 1087 cards had been funded. Funnel: issued (registry, not in this model) > FundedCards > PaymentUsers.
