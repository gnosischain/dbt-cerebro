api_governance_turnout_monthly

Monthly turnout trend (is_gip=1 only): a simple average of per-GIP turnout ratios, NOT population-weighted by eligible supply. Directional trend only -- use api_governance_turnout_latest for exact per-proposal figures. A month with no real GIP shows no row (rather than a misleading 0%), since the underlying model excludes non-GIP proposals.
