int_rpc_state_indexer_token_supply_daily

Daily supply and holder counts per token from the census. Publishes both supply definitions — supply_total from the contract's own totalSupply less the zero-address balance, and supply_holders from the observed holder balances — so the cutover from the transfer-derived series is measurable. Census-derived successor to int_execution_tokens_supply_holders_daily.

Columns

NameTypeDescription
dateDateCensus snapshot date, from the chain's canonical day anchor.
holdersUInt64Count of addresses with a balance greater than zero, excluding the zero address. Null for tokens not in the curated balances set.
supply_holdersFloat64Sum of observed holder balances excluding the zero address, normalized by decimals. Null for tokens that carry a supply scalar but are not in the curated balances set.
supply_totalFloat64The contract's totalSupply at the day's anchor, less the balance held at the zero address, normalized by decimals. Available for every whitelist token the census reads a scalar for.
symbolStringToken symbol, from the tokens_whitelist seed.
token_addressStringToken contract address, lowercased.
token_classStringToken classification, from the tokens_whitelist seed.
