fct_celo_gpay_cardholder_engagement

One row per Celo GP card, joining the card's own lifecycle to whether the person behind it is still economically alive on Celo at all. THE QUESTION THIS ANSWERS: the card-side tree can tell you a card stopped being used, but not why - and the two possible whys need opposite responses. A holder who left the chain is lost; a holder still transacting daily in their own wallet has simply stopped choosing the card, and that one is winnable. Until this model existed both looked identical (a churned card). Measured 2026-08-10 the split was not close: of cards dormant 7-30 days, 74% of holders were still active in-wallet; dormant 30+, 65%; funded but never spent, 83%. CLIPPED TO ONE HORIZON, for the same reason fct_celo_gpay_card_funnel is. The card signal (int_celo_gpay_activity_daily) and the wallet signal (int_celo_gpay_funder_wallet_daily) are separate incremental models built at different times; whichever lags is the binding constraint. observed_through is the LEAST of the two, never today(). If the wallet model lags a day, every holder would otherwise read as newly silent and the table would swing toward "gone". MAPPING IS PARTIAL AND SAYS SO. Only solo funders can be attributed unambiguously. Hub- or router-funded cards get wallet_address = NULL and land in a '__wallet_unmapped' segment - a coverage limit, NOT evidence of absence. Never read unmapped as gone.
