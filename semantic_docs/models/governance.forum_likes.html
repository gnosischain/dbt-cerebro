governance.forum_likes

Per-like edges from /user_actions.json (filter=1): one row per (post, liker) with the like's OWN created_at. This is what makes like timing answerable at all -- forum_posts.like_count is a scalar frozen at the topic's last bump, with no timestamp and no actor. Unlikes are not representable (the row simply stops being re-observed). acting_username is the crawled username, authoritative for "who liked this"; resolve the post's AUTHOR by joining forum_posts on post_id. Coverage is partial by nature: /user_actions.json 404s for 673 of 1169 users whose /u/{username}.json is a healthy 200, so roughly 74% of the directory's likes_given total is captured. The ingestor logs its capture rate every run rather than leaving the gap silent.
