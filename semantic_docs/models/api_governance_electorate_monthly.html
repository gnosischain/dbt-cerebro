api_governance_electorate_monthly

Electorate size per month measured two ways at once: how many PEOPLE voted and how much CAPITAL voted. The divergence between the two lines is the finding -- raw vote counts fell ~98.7% from 2022 to 2026, but median voting power per GIP is HIGHER now (137k vs 102k) and median voters per GIP (68) is back at the pre-airdrop baseline (79 in 2020). 2022 was an airdrop-farming bubble, not a healthy norm. Never publish the raw vote-count decline alone. GIP-only; months with no GIP are absent rather than zero. Medians, not means -- per-proposal participation is heavily skewed (max 1,196 voters).
