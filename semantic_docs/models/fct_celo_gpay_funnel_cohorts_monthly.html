fct_celo_gpay_funnel_cohorts_monthly

Cards grouped by the month they were ISSUED, with how far each cohort got through issued > funded > activated. Rollup of fct_celo_gpay_card_funnel. Cohorted and age-normalised on purpose. A single all-time "60% of cards get funded" is not a conversion rate on a product that is still ramping - the denominator is dominated by cards issued weeks ago, and the headline moves when issuance accelerates even if behaviour is identical. Cohorting removes the mix shift, the _7d/_30d windows remove the age spread within a cohort. Compare the windowed rates, not the _ever columns. funded_ever and activated_ever are honest counts as of the horizon but are NOT comparable across rows, because older cohorts have had longer to convert - they will always slope down toward the present and that slope is an artefact. The lag medians are completer-biased and no eligibility gate fixes it. They are taken over cards that DID convert, so a young cohort's median only sees its fast converters. Expect medians to drift up as a cohort matures; compare them only where cohort_complete_30d is true.
