stg_governance__forum_likes

One row per (post, liker) with the like's OWN timestamp, from /user_actions.json. This is the only source that can answer "how many likes did this post get DURING the voting window" -- forum_posts.like_count is a scalar frozen at the topic's last bump, with no timestamp and no actor. Hidden and deleted edges are KEPT so totals stay reconcilable against forum_posts; filter downstream where the intent is explicit. Unlikes are not representable (a row simply stops being re-observed). Captures ~74% of the directory's likes_given total -- /user_actions.json 404s for 673 of 1169 users whose profile endpoint is a healthy 200.
