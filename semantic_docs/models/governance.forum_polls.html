governance.forum_polls

Discourse poll options, one row per (post, poll, option) with its vote count. 189 polls across 180 topics back to 2020-11-02, 152 in GIP topics, and 75 carrying an "In Favour"/"Against" temperature check -- the only place in the corpus where one-person-one-vote community sentiment is recorded on a question that later gets a token-weighted Snapshot vote. Three traps, all load-bearing: option_votes = -1 means WITHHELD (results policy hides counts) and 0 is a real zero; close_at is a SCHEDULE never reconciled with reality (72 of 106 open polls have a lapsed close_at, 34 have none, none are future -- so "close_at < now()" reports every poll closed), `status` is the only open/closed signal; option_id is an md5 of the option TEXT so it recurs across unrelated topics and identifies an option only WITHIN a poll. The poll row carries no created_at -- date a poll by its host post.
