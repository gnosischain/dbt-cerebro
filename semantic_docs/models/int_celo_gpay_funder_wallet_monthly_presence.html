int_celo_gpay_funder_wallet_monthly_presence

One row per (month, funding wallet): earliest and latest Transfer leg that wallet had that month, plus leg counts. The batching-safe substrate for wallet TENURE. WHY A MONTHLY PRESENCE TABLE AND NOT A DIRECT min() PER WALLET. Tenure is `min(block_timestamp) per wallet` over all chain history, whose natural shape is a one-row-per-wallet table - but a table model CANNOT be batched. refresh.py re-runs the model per batch and a table materialisation does CREATE-OR-REPLACE each time, so every month would overwrite the last and the "minimum" would silently become the minimum of the final batch only. Folding presence per month into an incremental, partition-per-month table avoids that: each batch writes its own partition, and the min() is taken downstream over ~1k wallets x ~18 months instead of 2.7B raw logs. SCOPE IS WIDER THAN THE REST OF THE WALLET TREE ON PURPOSE. The floor is the start of coverage (2025-03), not the GP era (2026-01), because the question is what these wallets did BEFORE Gnosis Pay. And fee legs are COUNTED here, unlike int_celo_gpay_funder_wallet_daily: a CIP-64 gas receipt is not economic activity but it is proof the wallet existed and transacted that month. n_legs_nonfee carries the stricter reading. Left-censoring is handled downstream in fct_celo_gpay_wallet_tenure. This model reports what it observed and makes no claim that the first month it saw a wallet is the month that wallet began.
