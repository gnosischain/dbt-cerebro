stg_crawlers_data__defillama_prices

Staging for DefiLlama external USD prices. Dedupes append-only crawlers_data.defillama_prices to one row per (date, symbol). Raw rows may span chains; gnosis is preferred when a symbol appears on multiple chains. Feeds int_execution_token_prices_external_daily and the compare model; not wired into the production price hub yet.
