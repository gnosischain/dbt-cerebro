int_celo_gpay_funding_tx_envelopes

One row per inbound ERC-20 transfer onto a GP card Safe, with the funding transaction's envelope attached and a label-free funding_channel. funding_channel is a SHAPE classification, not an identity. CIP-64 (transaction_type 123) is Celo's fee-currency envelope and is used by most of the chain — it is necessary for MiniPay-style fee abstraction but not a MiniPay ID. Measured 2026-08-07: ~64% of all Celo txs and ~79% of calls to USDT/USDC were type 123; the discriminative signal for card funding is the composite of envelope + call shape + funder fan-out. feeCurrency is not in celo_execution.transactions. Channel values (assigned in this priority order): unknown — no matching row in celo_execution.transactions for the transfer's tx hash (should be rare once the funding window is fully indexed). mediated — the funding tx is NOT a direct EOA call of token.transfer() (from != funder, or to != token, or selector != transfer). Typical cases are Gnosis Safe execTransaction (0x6a761202) and routers. hub — the call IS a direct EOA transfer, but this funder funds two or more cards. Fan-out wins over envelope: even all-CIP-64 multi-card funders are hub. Includes the legacy 0xa0dd… onboarding hub (~250 cards, type 0). cip64_direct_solo — CIP-64 (type 123) + funder EOA called token.transfer() + that funder funds exactly one card. MiniPay-shaped; do not rename to "minipay" without an off-chain identity source. other_direct — same direct EOA transfer() shape and solo fan-out, but the envelope is not CIP-64 (type 0/2 — typically CELO gas). Consumed by fct_celo_gpay_card_funding (relationship-level channel, with mixed when transfers disagree) and fct_celo_gpay_card_funnel (first_fund_channel = channel of the card's earliest inbound transfer). Full-table rebuild; bounded by inbound transfer count (~6k).
