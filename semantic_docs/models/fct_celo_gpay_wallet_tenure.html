fct_celo_gpay_wallet_tenure

One row per GP card funding wallet: how long it had been active on Celo before it funded a card. The acquisition-vs-cross-sell question, answered per wallet. WHAT IT SETTLES. If wallets predate their card by months, GP on Celo is selling into MiniPay's installed base - a distribution result. If they appear days before the card, GP is bringing people on-chain - an acquisition result. Both produce the same headline card count, and the two were indistinguishable before this model. LEFT-CENSORING IS THE CENTRAL CAVEAT, NOT A ROUNDING ERROR. celo_execution starts at the L2 migration (2025-03-26) and Celo's L1 history is not in this warehouse, so first_seen_at means "first seen BY US". For any wallet already active at the migration it is a floor, not a birth date, and those wallets pile up at the window edge where they would otherwise manufacture a fake cohort of wallets born the day the L2 launched. is_left_censored marks them and days_before_first_funding is a LOWER BOUND for them, exact for everyone else. Never average that column without splitting on the flag, and never report a median tenure without reporting the censored share beside it.
