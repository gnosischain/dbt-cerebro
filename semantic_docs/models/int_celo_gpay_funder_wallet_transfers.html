int_celo_gpay_funder_wallet_transfers

All-token ERC-20 Transfer activity touching a GP card FUNDING WALLET on either side, with the counterparty classified against the GP universe. The wallet-side twin of int_celo_gpay_safe_transfers_alltoken: that model sees what the card does, this one sees what the cardholder does with the rest of their money. On 2026-08-05..08 the card boundary hid roughly ten times more transfer activity than it showed (18,157 wallet legs vs 1,674 card actions for the same people). Grain is (transfer, wallet-side): a transfer between two cardholder wallets yields two rows, one per side's own footprint. Do not dedupe across sides. amount / amount_usd / token_symbol / token_class are populated only for whitelisted (address-gated) tokens; everything else keeps amount_raw and leaves the rest NULL rather than guessing a decimal scale. The floor is the GP era (2026-01-01), not the wallet's whole life. Wallet TENURE needs history back to the L2 migration and is deliberately not modelled here.
