int_celo_gpay_module_mastercopies

Deterministic mastercopy allowlist: GP Zodiac module proxies created via the canonical ModuleProxyFactory from the Roles/Delay mastercopies GP provisions from. A CROSS-CHECK only (NOT a card-inclusion source) — verified on-chain to be a subset of the bridge-fingerprint universe (int_celo_gpay_safe_registry). Two Roles mastercopies are in play and both are needed for full coverage (verified 2026-08-04): roles_patched (0x732b9e9f…, current, source of every card since 2026-06-10, grows with issuance, 1452 proxies) and roles_pilot (0x9646fdad…, a CLOSED cohort used 2026-01-15..2026-06-12, 587 proxies). With both, the allowlist covers 100% of the fingerprint Safes; with roles_patched alone it silently missed the 51-card pilot cohort. DO NOT AGGREGATE THIS TABLE AS A GP CARD COUNT. roles_pilot is SHARED with unrelated projects, so of its 587 proxies only 286 are GP (51 wired the current settlement contract and are visible to int_celo_gpay_roles_modules; 235 wired the earlier 0xc4df5cac… bridge and are the unmodelled generation). The remaining ~301 belong to other projects entirely. That is the structural reason this model can never become an inclusion source, and why row counts here are not card counts. The GP subset is always the intersection with int_celo_gpay_roles_modules. The invariant worth checking against this model is fingerprint ⊆ mastercopy — every GP Roles proxy traces to a mastercopy here (held at 100% on 2026-08-04). It is intentionally a MANUAL probe rather than a dbt test, because a new mastercopy is legitimate GP activity, not a data defect. The query, and why the reverse direction must never be asserted, are in models/celo/AGENTS.md. Rows mean GP began issuing from a mastercopy this repo does not know — which has twice coincided with a new settlement contract, so treat it as a prompt to re-derive the card universe.
