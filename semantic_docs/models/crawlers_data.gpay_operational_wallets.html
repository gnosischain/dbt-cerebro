crawlers_data.gpay_operational_wallets

Gnosis Pay company-operated wallet addresses (treasury, relayers, settlement). Used purely as an EXCLUSION list so company wallets are not counted as users. These belong to a legal person, not a natural person, so they are out of GDPR scope - do NOT pseudonymize them: both call sites join against plaintext on-chain addresses, so hashing would break the exclusion for no privacy gain. Changes to this list silently move user counts. Treat edits as reviewable changes, not routine data updates.
