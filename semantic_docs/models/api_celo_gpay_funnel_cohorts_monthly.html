api_celo_gpay_funnel_cohorts_monthly

Card funnel by issuance cohort. `month` is the month the cards were ISSUED, not a month of activity, so the series does not read left-to-right as a timeline - each row describes what one group of cards went on to do. Plot the windowed rates. funded_ever/activated_ever slope down toward the present because recent cohorts have had less time, not because they convert worse. The rate columns are NULL for a cohort too young to judge, which is undefined rather than zero and should render as a gap, not a drop to the axis.
