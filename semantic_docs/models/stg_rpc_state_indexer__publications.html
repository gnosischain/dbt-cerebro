stg_rpc_state_indexer__publications

The published census attempt per (chain, job, target, day), selected in dbt from the indexer base tables. An attempt qualifies when it is verified (all batches verified, no failed observations, no terminal error) and its anchor is the chain's canonical finalized day anchor; when a target-day was published more than once (re-censused under a newer indexer config) the newest publication wins. Deliberately no config-hash gate.
