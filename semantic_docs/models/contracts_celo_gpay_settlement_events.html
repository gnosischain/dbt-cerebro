contracts_celo_gpay_settlement_events

Gnosis Pay's own settlement-contract events on Celo, decoded from celo_execution.logs for every address in seeds/celo_gpay_settlement_contracts. This is the AUTHORITATIVE record of card charges and treasury movement; int_celo_gpay_activity infers the same charges from ERC-20 Transfer direction instead. The two agree exactly when bound at the shared watermark least(max(activity.block_time), max(TokenPullSuccess.block_timestamp)) — enforced by celo_gpay_charges_match_payments on this model. That is the only external confirmation the transfer-based classifier has. Both settlement contracts live here in one model. decode_logs joins a log to its ABI on (address, topic0) rather than topic0 alone, so contracts with disjoint signatures cannot cross-match. Beyond the charges it exposes three things nothing else in the tree can see: failed charge attempts, which move no money and are therefore invisible to a transfer-derived model; the treasury leg (bridged cross-chain via the LayerZero USDT0 OFT, or transferred locally); and the CELO fee paid per settlement batch. HEAVY: a full-history decode in one query OOMs (ClickHouse Code 241). Rebuild via scripts/full_refresh/refresh.py in the monthly batches declared below, never a plain `dbt run --full-refresh`.

NameTypeDescription
block_numberUInt64Block containing the event.
block_timestampDateTimeOn-chain timestamp of the event.
contract_addressStringWhich settlement contract emitted the event. Stored BARE, with no 0x prefix, which is what decode_logs emits — joining it to seeds/celo_gpay_settlement_contracts.address (which is 0x-prefixed) needs replaceAll(lower(x),'0x','') on both sides or every row silently falls to the ELSE branch of your CASE.
decoded_paramsMap(String, String)Map of decoded event parameters. Shapes differ between the two contracts: every settlement_current event carries an extra leading `sender`. The one that matters most is TokenPullSuccess.rolesModifier, the card's Zodiac Roles module — it joins to int_celo_gpay_roles_modules and therefore identifies the card without going through transfers at all.
event_nameNullable(String)Decoded event name. TokenPullSuccess is the per-payment charge; TokenPullFailed and TokenPullFailedWithAmount are charge attempts that moved no money; SettlementBridged and SettlementTransferred are the treasury leg; NativeBridgeFeePaid is the CELO cost of a batch; BridgeMessageReceived and BridgeOftReceiptReceived are LayerZero receipts on settlement_current only. NULL means the emitting contract has no ABI loaded — see the no-undecoded-settlement-logs invariant.
log_indexUInt64Log index within the block.
transaction_hashStringTransaction hash (0x-prefixed).
transaction_indexUInt64Index of the transaction within its block.
