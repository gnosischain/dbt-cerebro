api_celo_gpay_settlement_cost_monthly

What it costs Gnosis Pay to settle Celo card charges, by month. Aggregates fct_celo_gpay_settlement_batches directly - the source is a few hundred rows, so a separate monthly fact would cost more to maintain than it saves. The headline is fee_usd_per_charge, which has fallen from 0.205 in March to 0.0074 in August as batching improved (1.3 charges per batch to 18.2). Only bridged batches pay anything; local settlement costs nothing beyond gas.
