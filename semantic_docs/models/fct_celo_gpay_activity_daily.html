fct_celo_gpay_activity_daily

Daily card-payment activity (active Safes, payment count, USD volume) plus BOTH funnel counts, which are different metrics: * funded — the card has ever RECEIVED money (any inbound action). * activated — the card has ever SPENT (settled a Payment to a settlement contract). Equals int_celo_gpay_wallets.is_activated. Funnel on 2026-08-05: 1817 issued (int_celo_gpay_safe_registry) > 1087 funded > 662 activated. RESTATED 2026-08-05. `cumulative_funded` previously counted from a card's first PAYMENT, so it tracked activation under the funded name and read 40% low (654 vs 1075). The original series is preserved as newly_activated/cumulative_activated. The date spine is the UNION of payment-activity days and funnel-transition days, not payment days alone: 9 days saw a card funded with nobody spending, and a payment-only spine dropped the 28 cards funded on them from cumulative_funded silently. Rows contributed only by the union carry active_users / total_payments / total_volume_usd = 0, which is the truth for those days — so do NOT read a 0 row as missing data. User grain is safe_address (on Celo a card = a Safe = the user). Current partial day is excluded. Whitelisted tokens only (USDT/USDC in practice — see scope note).

Columns

NameTypeDescription
active_usersUInt64Distinct Safes that made a payment on this date. 0 on a date present only because a card was funded that day (see the spine note above).
cumulative_activatedUInt64Running total of ever-spent Safes through this date; 654 on 2026-08-05. This is the series cumulative_funded carried before the restatement. Always <= cumulative_funded, since a card cannot spend before it is funded.
cumulative_fundedUInt64Running total of ever-funded Safes through this date. Reaches 1075 on 2026-08-05 (1087 all-time, less cards first funded today, which is excluded).
dateDateCalendar date.
newly_activatedUInt64Safes whose first-ever payment fell on this date.
newly_fundedUInt64Safes that RECEIVED money for the first time on this date.
total_paymentsUInt64Number of payments settled on this date. 0 on a funding-only date.
total_volume_usdFloat64USD payment volume on this date (USDC + USDT). 0 on a funding-only date.
