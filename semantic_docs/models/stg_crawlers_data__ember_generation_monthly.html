stg_crawlers_data__ember_generation_monthly

Ember monthly electricity data ("generation" release): one row per area x month x electricity source, metrics as columns, read with FINAL from the full-reload raw table. iso3_code is empty for Ember's aggregate regions (World, EU, G20, ...). The ESG chain reads electricity_source 'Total generation' and its emissions_intensity_gco2e_kwh.
