int_execution_gpay_spender_accounts

Bridge from Spender Spend.account addresses to card-Safe identities, one row per account ever debited by a Spender module. Spend.account is a per-card module ENABLED ON the card Safe, not the Safe itself (the Safe emits ExecutionFromModuleSuccess(module=account) in the same spend transaction). The enabling Safe comes from int_execution_safes_module_events (event_kind='enabled_module', target_address = account); the mapping is 1:1 over full history. canonical_safe collapses June 2026 migrated OLD Safes onto their NEW Safe via int_execution_gpay_safe_canonical. Accounts with no enable event (decode lag) keep safe_address NULL so consumers pick their gate.
