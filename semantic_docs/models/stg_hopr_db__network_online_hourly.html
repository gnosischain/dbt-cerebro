stg_hopr_db__network_online_hourly

Hourly count of online HOPR nodes from the network.hoprnet.org dashboard's own history, ingested by click-runner's hopr-network ingestor. Reads FINAL because the source is a ReplacingMergeTree(ingested_at) and the backfill re-inserts every hour it already holds. This is the only HOPR series with multi-year history -- every other feed begins the day its ingestor first ran -- and the only measure of how many nodes are actually up rather than how many ever registered on-chain. The series has substantial hour-level gaps, so consumers must count observed hours rather than assume 24 per day; fct_hopr_network_health_daily exposes that as hours_observed. dufour only: the prober was never ported to jura/v4.
