int_governance_proposal_topic_links

The unified forum-topic <-> Snapshot-proposal bridge, one row per (proposal, topic). Everything phase-related joins through here instead of re-deriving a bridge. Union of two signals: matching GIP number in both titles (links 133 proposals) and a post pasting the proposal's 0x URL (links 95, and reaches non-GIP proposals the number match cannot). Union is 137 of 253 proposals (54%), effectively all 134 GIPs, across 265 pairs. The proposal's own `discussion` URL is deliberately NOT a third signal -- it parses for only 89 and is a subset of the above. MANY-TO-MANY BY DESIGN: re-balloted GIPs (74/87/91/134) give a topic several proposals.
