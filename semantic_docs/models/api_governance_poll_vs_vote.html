api_governance_poll_vs_vote

The forum's headcount verdict against the token verdict on the SAME question. The only comparison in the layer between two genuinely SEPARATE populations -- a forum temperature check (one person one vote, no token needed) and the Snapshot ballot that followed. head_minus_weight_against on api_governance_proposal_direction compares headcount to capital within ONE ballot, i.e. the same voters weighted twice; here the voters are different people. It sometimes reverses outright: GIP-120 forum 58% against -> Snapshot 0.05% against by VP; GIP-128 37% -> 0.01%; GIP-119 the opposite at 13% -> 98.7%. A blended contestation score would hide that those are opposite phenomena. FILTER is_pre_vote_check for the honest subset (130 of 142 pairs). 2 polls ran during voting and 10 AFTER the ballot closed -- a post-close poll is a retrospective, not a temperature check. Those rows are kept with their phase exposed so the exclusion is visible rather than silent. GRAIN (poll, proposal), MANY-TO-MANY both ways -- a topic hosts several named polls and links to several proposals when a GIP is re-balloted, so GIP-120 legitimately appears more than once. Never aggregate without deciding which poll you mean. Poll totals are small (roughly 10-65 votes): directional, not precise.
