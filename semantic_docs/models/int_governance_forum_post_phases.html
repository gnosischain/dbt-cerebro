int_governance_forum_post_phases

Every forum post placed in the lifecycle phase of a proposal its topic links to: pre_discussion / pre_vote / voting / post_close. Turns "opinion moved at hour 40" into "here is what was said at hour 40" -- align hours_from_vote_open against api_governance_vote_trajectory.hour_offset. GRAIN IS (post, proposal), NOT post: a post in a topic linked to two proposals yields two rows and its phase can legitimately differ between them, so counting posts without grouping by proposal_id double-counts. Coverage is partial (137 of 253 proposals); posts in unlinked topics have no row at all.

Columns

NameTypeDescription
has_questionBoolPost contains '?' after [quote] blocks are stripped. A friction proxy needing no sentiment model. Quote-stripping matters: 11% of posts quote someone else, and a question inside a quotation is not the author's.
hours_from_vote_openInt64Hours from start_at; negative for posts before voting opened.
is_short_proceduralBoolUnder 120 chars ex-quotes -- "cc @x", moderation. 19% of the corpus.
phaseStringDerived from the proposal's own created_at/start_at/end_at, NOT from the DAO's declared phase-1/2/3 tags (those cover only ~21% of topics). post_close is a signal in its own right, not leftover noise.
post_idUInt64
post_like_count_currentUInt32Current-state scalar frozen at the topic's last bump. For likes that can be attributed to a phase, use stg_governance__forum_likes.
proposal_idString
