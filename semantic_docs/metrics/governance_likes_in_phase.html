governance_likes_in_phase

Likes counted by the LIKE's own timestamp, grouped by proposal phase -- the real "endorsement during the vote" figure. Depends on the per-like edge table (forum_likes); it is NOT derivable from forum_posts.like_count, which is a scalar frozen at the topic's last bump with no timestamp. Likes are the only signal available from people who never voted and never posted. Captures ~74% of the forum's likes_given total -- see stg_governance__forum_likes for why.
