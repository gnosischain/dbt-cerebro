governance_head_vs_weight_divergence

Against-share by voter COUNT minus against-share by voting POWER, for one proposal. Positive means small holders were more opposed than capital; negative means capital was more opposed than the crowd. The sharpest read on whether a decision had broad consent or merely sufficient weight. READ IT PER PROPOSAL -- group by gip_number. Averaging it across proposals is not a defined population statistic (each proposal has a different electorate), which is why this is candidate rather than approved. Filter voters >= 30 and is_gip = 1; a divergence over a handful of voters is noise and 119 of 253 proposals are non-GIP spam. Only meaningful where directional = true (basic and single-choice ballots, 96.8% of proposals) -- ranked-choice is NULL.
