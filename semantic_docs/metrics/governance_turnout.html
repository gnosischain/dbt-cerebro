governance_turnout

Per-GIP voting-power turnout (is_gip=1 only -- excludes spam/announcement Snapshot activity, which is ~47% of all proposals but averages near-0% turnout and would otherwise dilute the signal) -- total_vp cast / eligible GNO supply (circulating on both chains, plus staked GNO when the proposal's own strategies include it as an eligible pool). Group by gip_number/category/outcome. Pre-2022 ('gno'-strategy era) figures are directional -- only the current getGnoVotingPower contract was independently audited to confirm it's a simple balance passthrough.
