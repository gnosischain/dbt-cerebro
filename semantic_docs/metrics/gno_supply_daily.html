gno_supply_daily

Daily GNO token supply derived from on-chain state (rpc-state-indexer), broken down by supply-category label; history from 2020-08-23. Group or filter by the `label` dimension. Three labels are mutually exclusive components of minted supply (Ethereum Circ., Gnosis Circ., Non-Circ.); 'Total Circ. Supply' is DERIVED as Ethereum Circ. + Gnosis Circ., so an unfiltered sum across labels double-counts the circulating portion and is not a meaningful total. For circulating supply, filter label = 'Total Circ. Supply'. Units are GNO token quantities.

Label: GNO Supply (daily, by label)

Module: rpc_state_indexer

Status: approved

Measure: crawlers_data_gno_supply_daily__supply_value

Allowed dimensions: label, date

Supported time grains: day, week, month, quarter, year
