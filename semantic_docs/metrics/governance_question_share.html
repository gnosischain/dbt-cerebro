governance_question_share

Share of posts asking something rather than asserting, measured on text with [quote] blocks stripped -- a friction proxy that needs no sentiment model. READ IT PER (proposal, phase). Averaging a share across proposals is not population-weighted (each has a different post count), which is why this is candidate rather than approved; weight by posts if you need a real aggregate.
