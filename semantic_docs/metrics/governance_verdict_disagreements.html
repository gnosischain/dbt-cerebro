governance_verdict_disagreements

Count of (poll, proposal) pairs where the forum temperature check and the token-weighted Snapshot vote landed on OPPOSITE sides of 50%. The headline cases: GIP-120 (forum 58% against, Snapshot 0.05% against by voting power) and GIP-128 (37% versus 0.01%) were voted down by the community and passed nearly unopposed by token holders; GIP-119 ran the other way. Filter is_pre_vote_check. Grain is (poll, proposal) and is many-to-many, so a re-balloted GIP can contribute more than one pair -- this counts pairs, not proposals.
