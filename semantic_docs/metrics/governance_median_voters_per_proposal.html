governance_median_voters_per_proposal

Median number of distinct addresses voting on a GIP, by month. Median rather than mean because per-proposal participation is heavily skewed by airdrop-era ballots (max 1,196 voters). ALWAYS read alongside governance_median_vp_per_proposal: headcount fell from 581/GIP in 2022 to 68 in 2026 while voting power per GIP ROSE, so the headcount series alone reads as collapse when it is really an airdrop bubble deflating back to the 2020-21 baseline. Months with no GIP are absent, not zero.
