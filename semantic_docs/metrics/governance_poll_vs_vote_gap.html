governance_poll_vs_vote_gap

Forum temperature-check against-share MINUS the Snapshot against-share by voting power, per (poll, proposal). POSITIVE means the community was more opposed than the capital that actually decided it; NEGATIVE means capital was more opposed than the community. Unlike governance_head_vs_weight_divergence -- which weights the SAME ballot two ways -- this compares two genuinely different populations answering the same question, so it is the only measure of community-versus-tokenholder disagreement. ALWAYS filter is_pre_vote_check: 10 polls ran after the ballot closed and are retrospectives, not temperature checks. Read per proposal; poll totals are small (roughly 10-65 votes) so this is directional, not precise -- hence candidate.
